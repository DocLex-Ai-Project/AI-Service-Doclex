LAW: Indian Contract Act, 1872

Definition:
- Proposal: When a person expresses willingness to do or not do something to obtain consent.
- Acceptance: When the other person agrees to the proposal.
- Promise: Proposal + Acceptance.
- Consideration: Something given in return for a promise.
- Agreement: Promise with consideration.
- Contract: Agreement enforceable by law.

Validity Conditions:
- Offer + Acceptance must exist
- Lawful consideration required
- Free consent required
- Parties must be competent

Invalid Cases:
- No consideration → VOID
- Illegal object → VOID
- No consent → VOIDABLE

Risk Rules:
- Missing acceptance → INVALID CONTRACT
- No consideration → HIGH RISK
- Illegal terms → HIGH RISK
