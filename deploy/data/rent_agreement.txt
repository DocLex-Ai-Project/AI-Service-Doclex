DOCUMENT: Rent Agreement (India)

REQUIRED CLAUSES:
- Tenant and landlord details
- Rent amount
- Security deposit
- Duration of lease
- Maintenance responsibility
- Termination notice period

RULES:
- Agreement must be signed by both parties
- Stamp duty may be required

RISK DETECTION:
- No duration → INVALID agreement
- No rent amount → incomplete contract
- No notice period → dispute risk
