ACT: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)

SECTION: 1
TITLE: Short title, commencement and application

CONTENT:
Defines applicability of BNS across India and outside India for Indian citizens.

KEY POINTS:
- Applies within India
- Applies to Indian citizens abroad
- Includes cyber offences targeting India

RISK:
- Cross-border crimes are punishable


SECTION: DEFINITIONS

TITLE: Important Legal Definitions

KEY TERMS:
- Document: Includes written, digital, electronic records
- Fraudulently: Act done with intent to deceive
- Dishonestly: Causing wrongful gain or loss
- Illegal: Anything prohibited by law

RISK:
- Fake digital documents → HIGH RISK
- Misuse of legal terms → HIGH RISK


SECTION: 4
TITLE: Punishments

CONTENT:
Types of punishments under BNS.

KEY POINTS:
- Death penalty
- Life imprisonment
- Simple / rigorous imprisonment
- Fine
- Community service

RISK:
- Severe crimes → extreme penalties


SECTION: 5
TITLE: Commutation of sentence

CONTENT:
Government can reduce or change punishment.

KEY POINTS:
- Central/State government authority
- No consent required

RISK:
- Legal authority required


SECTION: 8
TITLE: Fine and imprisonment rules

CONTENT:
Defines fine limits and default imprisonment.

KEY POINTS:
- Fine can be unlimited but reasonable
- Default imprisonment allowed
- Depends on fine amount

RISK:
- Non-payment → imprisonment


SECTION: 14
TITLE: Act done by mistake of fact

CONTENT:
No offence if person believes in good faith they are bound by law.

KEY POINTS:
- Mistake of fact allowed
- Not mistake of law

RISK:
- Wrong legal assumption → NOT protected


SECTION: 17
TITLE: Act justified by law

CONTENT:
No offence if action is legally justified.

KEY POINTS:
- Good faith protection
- Legal authority required

RISK:
- Misuse of justification → legal issue


SECTION: 18
TITLE: Accident

CONTENT:
No offence if act is accidental without criminal intent.

KEY POINTS:
- Must be lawful act
- Proper care required

RISK:
- Negligence → becomes offence


SECTION: 19
TITLE: Preventing greater harm

CONTENT:
Act causing harm is not offence if done to prevent greater harm.

KEY POINTS:
- Good faith required
- Must be necessary action

RISK:
- Misuse → legal liability


SECTION: 20
TITLE: Child under 7

CONTENT:
Child below 7 cannot commit offence.

RISK:
- No criminal liability


SECTION: 21
TITLE: Child 7–12

CONTENT:
Child not liable if lacks maturity.

RISK:
- Case-based judgement


SECTION: 22
TITLE: Unsound mind

CONTENT:
No offence if person mentally incapable.

RISK:
- Must prove mental condition


SECTION: 28
TITLE: Invalid consent

CONTENT:
Consent is invalid if:
- Given under fear
- Given under misunderstanding
- Person not mentally capable

RISK:
- Forced agreement → INVALID
- Fraud consent → HIGH RISK
SECTION: 316
TITLE: Criminal Breach of Trust

CONTENT:
A person commits criminal breach of trust if they are entrusted with property and dishonestly misappropriate or use it for personal gain or violate legal directions.

KEY POINTS:
- Property must be entrusted
- Dishonest intention required
- Misuse or conversion of property
- Violation of legal contract or law

PUNISHMENT:
- Up to 5 years imprisonment or fine
- Up to 7 years if special roles (carrier, clerk, etc.)
- Up to life imprisonment for public servant or banker

RISK:
- Misuse of entrusted funds → HIGH RISK
- Violation of agreement → HIGH RISK
- Employee fund misuse → HIGH RISK

---

SECTION: 318
TITLE: Cheating

CONTENT:
Cheating involves deceiving a person to deliver property or act in a way that causes damage or loss.

KEY POINTS:
- Deception required
- Dishonest intention
- Inducing delivery of property
- Causing harm (financial, reputation, etc.)

PUNISHMENT:
- Up to 3 years imprisonment or fine
- Up to 5 years if legal duty violated
- Up to 7 years for serious cheating

RISK:
- False promises → HIGH RISK
- Fake representation → HIGH RISK
- Fraudulent agreements → HIGH RISK

---

SECTION: 336
TITLE: Forgery

CONTENT:
Forgery means creating false documents or electronic records to cause harm, support false claims, or commit fraud.

KEY POINTS:
- False document creation
- Intent to deceive
- Use for fraud or contract

PUNISHMENT:
- Up to 2 years imprisonment or fine
- Up to 7 years if used for cheating
- Up to 3 years if harms reputation

RISK:
- Fake signature → HIGH RISK
- Altered contract → HIGH RISK
- Fake digital record → HIGH RISK

---

SECTION: 337
TITLE: Forgery of Public Documents

CONTENT:
Forgery of official records, identity documents, or government-issued documents.

KEY POINTS:
- Court records
- Aadhaar, voter ID
- Public registers

PUNISHMENT:
- Up to 7 years imprisonment and fine

RISK:
- Fake ID → HIGH RISK
- Fake government documents → HIGH RISK

---

SECTION: 338
TITLE: Forgery of Valuable Security

CONTENT:
Forgery involving financial instruments like securities, wills, or authority documents.

KEY POINTS:
- Financial fraud
- Fake will or transfer documents

PUNISHMENT:
- Life imprisonment or up to 10 years + fine

RISK:
- Financial document fraud → VERY HIGH RISK

---

SECTION: 339
TITLE: Possession of Forged Document

CONTENT:
Possessing forged documents with intent to use them as genuine.

KEY POINTS:
- Knowledge of forgery
- Intent to use

PUNISHMENT:
- Up to 7 years or life depending on type

RISK:
- Holding fake documents → HIGH RISK

---

SECTION: 340
TITLE: Using Forged Document

CONTENT:
Using forged documents knowingly as genuine.

KEY POINTS:
- Knowledge of forgery
- Intentional use

PUNISHMENT:
- Same as forgery

RISK:
- Using fake agreement → HIGH RISK

---

SECTION: 341
TITLE: Counterfeit Seal or Instrument

CONTENT:
Making or possessing tools used for forgery.

KEY POINTS:
- Fake seals
- Forgery tools

PUNISHMENT:
- Up to life imprisonment or 7 years

RISK:
- Possession of fake seal → HIGH RISK
