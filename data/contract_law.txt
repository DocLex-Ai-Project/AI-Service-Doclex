LAW: Indian Contract Act 1872

VALID CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS:
- Offer and Acceptance
- Lawful Consideration
- Free Consent
- Competent Parties
- Lawful Object

RULES:
- Agreement without consideration is VOID
- Contract with minor is INVALID
- Coercion or fraud makes contract VOIDABLE

IMPORTANT LEGAL PRINCIPLES:
- Both parties must agree freely
- Terms must be clear and lawful

CLAUSES REQUIRED IN CONTRACT:
- Payment terms
- Termination clause
- Liability clause
- Jurisdiction clause

RISK DETECTION:
- Missing consideration → HIGH RISK
- Illegal purpose → INVALID CONTRACT
- No termination clause → MEDIUM RISK
