DOCUMENT: Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

REQUIRED CLAUSES:
- Definition of confidential information
- Obligations of receiving party
- Duration of confidentiality
- Exclusions
- Remedies for breach

RULES:
- NDA must clearly define what is confidential
- Time duration must be specified

RISK DETECTION:
- No duration → weak agreement
- No penalty clause → LOW enforceability
- Vague definitions → HIGH risk
